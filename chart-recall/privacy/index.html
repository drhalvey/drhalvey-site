Chart Recall privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Chart Recall is an iPhone app that helps anaesthetists keep a private record of their own anaesthetic charts, so that when a patient returns they can see what happened last time. It is published by Dr Edward Halvey, Subiaco, Western Australia (the developer).

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. The developer never receives, sees or stores any information entered into the app. The App Store privacy label for Chart Recall is "Data Not Collected".

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores, on your iPhone only, what you capture and confirm: photographs of anaesthetic chart pages and patient identification stickers, the text read from them, the patient details (name, date of birth, hospital record number), and the summary card for each anaesthetic (date, hospital, procedure, surgeon, technique, airway, drugs, problems and your notes).

Text is read from the photographs on the phone using Apple's on-device text recognition. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device language model may be used to fill gaps in the summary. Both run entirely on the phone; nothing is sent to Apple, the developer or anyone else. You can turn Apple Intelligence off in Settings.

The data is kept in the app's own storage with iOS complete file protection, so it cannot be read while the phone is locked. It is excluded from iCloud Backup and from computer backups, the app never uses iCloud or any other cloud service, and photographs taken in the app are not saved to the Photos library.

How information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to share it. The "Share as PDF" button on a saved chart opens the iOS share sheet with a PDF of that chart; where it goes is your decision. The app never sends anything on its own.

Retention and deletion

Charts are kept until you delete them. You can delete a single chart, a patient and all their charts, or everything at once in Settings, and you can set the app to delete charts older than 1, 2, 5 or 7 years automatically. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Device permissions

Camera: to photograph charts and stickers. Photos: only through the system photo picker, which shows the app only the pictures you select. Face ID or Touch ID: to lock the app, if you keep that setting on. None of these are used for any other purpose.

Your responsibilities

You, as the health service provider, are responsible for the patient information you record with this app, including patient consent where it is required, your obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and any hospital or practice policies that apply to you. Chart Recall is a personal memory aid. It is not a medical record and gives no clinical advice. The developer is not a party to the information you record and does not act on your behalf.

Children

Chart Recall is a professional tool for medical practitioners and is not directed at children.
