What the app is

Billing Sheet is a record-keeping tool. It lets you note the anaesthesia services you provided, attach patient identification details, and email a summary to your practice. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat anything, and it does not submit claims to Medicare, a health fund or anyone else.

Item numbers are yours to check
