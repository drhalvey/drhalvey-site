Billing Sheet privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Billing Sheet is an iPhone app that helps anaesthetists keep a private record of the services they provided during a list (labour ward, endoscopy, orthopaedic or any other) and email that record to their practice for billing. It is published by Dr Edward Halvey, Subiaco, Western Australia (the developer).

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. The developer never receives, sees or stores any information entered into the app. The App Store privacy label for Billing Sheet is "Data Not Collected".

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores, on your iPhone only, the information you enter: a photograph of a patient identification sticker, the details read from it (name, date of birth, hospital record and admission numbers, Medicare number, the fund line, the treating doctor), the services you record, their times, Medicare Benefits Schedule item numbers, notes and fee estimates, any fee estimate letters you make, and your own settings and item sets. If you import a theatre list, the details read from it are stored the same way.

Before each email is sent and before anything is deleted, the app keeps a restore point on the phone (the list, its sticker photographs and the PDFs as sent), so a lost or mistaken change can be recovered. Restore points stay on the phone, are never shared unless you resend from one, and are deleted automatically one retention period after the list they belong to. "Delete all sessions and patients" deletes them too.

This data is kept in the app's own storage on the device. It is excluded from iCloud Backup and from computer backups, and the app never uses iCloud or any other cloud service. It is not stored in the Photos library.

How information leaves your phone

Information leaves the phone only when you send or share it yourself. The app does not send anything on its own.

  • The billing email. At the end of a list the app opens the Apple Mail compose sheet (or, if Mail is not set up, the share sheet) with the email text and one PDF per patient. You review it and you press Send; the email goes from your own mail account to the recipient you have set, and to a copy address if you added one.
  • Files you choose to share from the share sheet: an encrypted backup file (locked with a passphrase you choose), a fee estimate letter for a patient, a spreadsheet of your fee estimates, or, only if you turn on "Export lists to SIOP" in Settings, an unencrypted list file for the SIOP Mac app.
  • Apple Watch, if you use the watch app: the watch is sent the patient's initials, the case type, the times and, unless you hide amounts, the estimated fee for the case in progress and the next case on the list. Nothing else about the patient goes to the watch.
  • Lock screen and home screen: the case in progress shows the patient's initials, the case type and the time, and the home screen widget shows the estimated fee unless you hide amounts. Full names are never shown there.

Retention and deletion

After you send a list, the app asks you to confirm that the email arrived. A confirmed list is deleted automatically a set time after you confirm it (90 days by default, changeable in Settings), and its restore point the same time after that. Lists you have not confirmed are kept until you delete them, so nothing is lost before it has been billed. Sticker photographs are kept with the list unless you turn on "Delete sticker photos once I confirm". You can delete any session by swiping, or delete everything at once with "Delete all sessions and patients" in Settings. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Device permissions

Camera: to photograph the sticker. Photos: only through the system photo picker, which shows you the picture you select and nothing else. Face ID or Touch ID: to lock the app, if you keep that setting on. Notifications: only if you turn on the reminder to email unsent lists. None of these are used for any other purpose.

Your responsibilities

You, as the health service provider, are responsible for the patient information you record and send with this app, including your obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and any hospital or practice policies that apply to you. The developer is not a party to that information and does not act on your behalf.

Children

Billing Sheet is a professional tool for medical practitioners and is not directed at children.
