Billing Sheet privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Billing Sheet is an iPhone app that helps anaesthetists keep a private record of the services they provided during a labour ward session and email that record to their practice for billing. It is published by Dr Edward Halvey, Subiaco, Western Australia (the developer).

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. The developer never receives, sees or stores any information entered into the app. The App Store privacy label for Billing Sheet is "Data Not Collected".

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores, on your iPhone only, the information you enter: a photograph of a patient identification sticker, the details read from it (name, date of birth, hospital record numbers, Medicare number, fund class, obstetrician), the services you record, their times and Medicare Benefits Schedule item numbers, and your editable service presets.

This data is kept in the app's own storage on the device. It is excluded from iCloud Backup and from computer backups, and the app never uses iCloud or any other cloud service. It is not stored in the Photos library.

How information leaves your phone

The only way information leaves the phone is the email you choose to send. When you end a session the app opens the Apple Mail compose sheet with the email text and one PDF per patient. You review it and you press Send; the email goes from your own mail account to the recipient you have set. The app does not send anything itself and does not keep a copy of the email.

Retention and deletion

By default the sticker photograph is deleted as soon as the session email has been sent, and emailed sessions are deleted automatically 90 days after sending. Both settings can be changed in the app. Sessions that were never emailed are kept until you delete them. You can delete any session by swiping, or delete everything at once with "Delete all sessions and patients" in Settings. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Device permissions

Camera: to photograph the sticker. Photos: only through the system photo picker, which shows you the picture you select and nothing else. Face ID or Touch ID: to lock the app, if you keep that setting on. None of these are used for any other purpose.

Your responsibilities

You, as the health service provider, are responsible for the patient information you record and send with this app, including your obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and any hospital or practice policies that apply to you. The developer is not a party to that information and does not act on your behalf.

Children

Billing Sheet is a professional tool for medical practitioners and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the app will show it again on next launch. Questions or complaints: eh@metroanaes.com.au. If you are not satisfied with the response you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
