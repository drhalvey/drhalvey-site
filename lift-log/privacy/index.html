Lift Log privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Lift Log is a gym workout log with an AI coach for iPhone, with an Apple Watch rest timer. It records your workouts, builds a training plan from your goals and the time you have, and suggests the load, reps and effort for each set. It is published by Dr Edward Halvey, Subiaco, Western Australia (the developer).

In short

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your workout history, plan and profile are stored on your own devices. When the free built-in AI writes a coaching reply or a plan, the information it needs is sent to the developer's server and on to Google's Gemini AI service to produce that reply, and is not kept by the developer.

What the app stores on your devices

Your workouts and sets, routines, exercises you create, body measurements you enter, your settings, and the training profile you give during setup: your goals, experience, equipment, any injuries or movements to avoid, age and bodyweight. This is kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. The app does not use iCloud. If you enter your own AI service key, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone only.

Apple Watch

The iPhone sends the current rest timer and set to the Lift Log app on your Apple Watch using Apple's Watch Connectivity, which runs only between your own paired devices. The watch keeps no workout history.

Health data

On Apple Watch, Lift Log can run a strength training workout while a workout is open on your iPhone, so that the rest timer stays on screen. If you allow it, the session is saved as a Traditional Strength Training workout in Apple Health, and your heart rate is read during the session. This data is handled by Apple Health on your devices; the app does not keep its own copy, never sends it to the developer or to any AI service, and never uses it for advertising or marketing. You can decline the Health permission, turn "Keep screen on (workout)" off in the watch app, or change access at any time in the Health app on your iPhone.

How the AI coach uses your information

The coach can run in three ways, chosen in Settings.

Apple Intelligence (on-device). On iPhones that support it, set-by-set coaching runs on the iPhone itself and nothing leaves your device for that purpose.

Lift Log AI (the free built-in AI). When this is used, for each coaching turn or plan the app sends a request containing what the coach needs: your training profile, the current workout and recent history for the exercises involved, your coach notes and the message you typed or tapped. The request goes to the developer's server (Supabase, hosted in Sydney, Australia), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API, and if Gemini is unavailable to Groq, to generate the reply. The developer's server does not store the content of your requests or the replies. To keep the free service fair, the app sends a random identifier created when the app is installed; the server keeps only a count of requests per identifier per day, deleted after 30 days. This identifier is not linked to your name, Apple ID or device. The server's hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability.

Google provides the Gemini API under its own terms. On the free tier Google may retain requests and use them to improve its products, and they may be reviewed by people at Google. Please do not type names or other identifying details into the coach. Groq is used only as a fallback and processes requests under its own terms.

Your own AI account. If you choose another provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Google with your own key), requests go directly from your iPhone to that provider under its terms, not through the developer.

If no AI is available, a built-in rule engine on your iPhone still calls every set.

Notifications

The app can show a local notification when your rest time is up. These are scheduled on your devices and are not sent through any server.

Retention and deletion

Everything stored on your devices is kept until you delete it. You can delete all data with "Delete all data" in Settings, or by deleting the app. Export your workouts first with Export & Import Data if you want a copy. The anonymous daily request counts on the developer's server are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Not medical advice

Lift Log gives general training suggestions. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. Stop and seek advice from a health professional if you have pain or a medical condition.

Children

Lift Log is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
